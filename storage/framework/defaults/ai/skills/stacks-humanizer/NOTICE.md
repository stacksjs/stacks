Stacks uses its own namespaced entrypoint and keeps all 26 patterns in a
disclosed reference. The adaptation adds Stacks file-preservation guidance,
related-skill routing, and the project's punctuation rule. That rule takes
precedence over the upstream exception for voice samples containing dashes.
Examples use regular hyphens. The authorship caveat states an editorial
criterion rather than inferring authorship from a document's date.

This snapshot is bundled locally. Installing or invoking the skill does not
download upstream content or execute upstream scripts.
