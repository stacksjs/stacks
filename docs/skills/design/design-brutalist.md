# Design: brutalist

`stacks-design-brutalist` · Design · model-invoked

An aesthetic preset for industrial and brutalist work: Swiss typography, sharp
high contrast, rigid modular grids, monospace telemetry and experimental
analog-degradation effects. Suits data-heavy dashboards and editorial sites.

## When to reach for it

- Designing a data-heavy dashboard
- Portfolio
- Editorial site that needs Swiss typography
- Sharp high contrast
- Rigid modular grids
- Monospace telemetry
- Experimental analog-degradation effects
